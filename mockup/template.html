Leadership · Who is winning, and is the market concentrating?

Chinese labs have processed more OpenRouter tokens than US labs every week since April 27

Reading it right: this is traffic routed through OpenRouter, not the whole market. Apps that call OpenAI, Anthropic or Google directly are invisible here. "Everyone else" combines labs outside China and the US with stealth models and the long tail outside each day's top 50.

Pricing & finance · Does cheaper win? Where is the money?

China wins the tokens; US labs still win the money

Estimated spend = tokens x list price, blending input and output 15:1 (OpenRouter's own study reports about 6K prompt vs 400 completion tokens per request). It's before caching discounts, so it's an upper bound: use it for shares, not dollar totals. Under Artificial Analysis's 3:1 blend, China's September spend share is 36.5% and the US's is 63.5%.

AI platform team · Which models give the best quality for the price?

The most-used models are cheap mid-scorers, not the benchmark leaders

Routing, not taste: benchmarks score one setting per model (for example "Max" reasoning), while usage counts every setting. Bulk jobs such as classification, the largest task by requests this week, can rationally use cheap models. Benchmarks: Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai) via OpenRouter, as of Oct 17, 2026.

Competitive intelligence · Where do the threats come from?

China's lead is broad: it holds without DeepSeek and under the worst-case count

Country means the developer's stated headquarters, from each lab's own legal or about page (`data/reference/labs.csv`). It's not where a model is hosted, and not where a base model it was tuned from came from.

Product & GTM · Do launches stick? What drives demand?

Models churn within weeks; labs keep most of their users

Launches are model families that first entered the daily top 50 on or after Feb 1, 2025, peaked at 1% or more of daily tokens, and have at least 13 weeks of data. Stealth models are excluded, because they "end" when renamed at launch. A lab that drops out of the top 50 counts as zero.
